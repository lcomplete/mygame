Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: a production animation sprite atlas for the premium 2D side-scrolling wuxia action game "Sever the Decree".
Primary request: beautifully redesign the protagonist Ying Seventeen into an exceptionally polished hand-painted ink-and-cel game character.
Subject: a lean adult Chinese shadow swordsman, alert narrow eyes visible above a dark teal face mask, wrapped hood, layered midnight teal short robes, small aged bronze shoulder guard and belt clasp, ivory forearm and shin bindings, dark fitted trousers and boots. A long split vermilion scarf flows left behind him. One sharp single-edged silver dao with a dark grip, and a dark scabbard at his waist. Distinct elegant silhouette, grounded anatomy about five heads tall, determined and agile. Face and scarf highly readable at small size.
Style: premium 2D hand-painted game animation, expressive crisp dark ink contours, broad cel-shaded shadow shapes, tactile cloth folds, restrained moonlit teal highlights and warm antique-gold metal. Richly designed but no excessive tiny detailing. Not pixel art, not a 3D render, not a flat geometric icon.
Layout is absolutely critical: square transparent canvas, EXACTLY 4 columns and 4 rows of equal square cells, 16 full-body sprites total. Each sprite is isolated within its own cell with generous transparent margins. NO painted background, NO checkerboard, NO labels, grid lines, numbers, frames, ground shadows, decorative panels or text. GENUINE alpha transparency.
Orthographic side view facing RIGHT in every cell, same camera, anatomy, costume and body scale throughout. The BODY origin is centered horizontally in every cell, do not center the bounding box of his scarf or sword. Standing sole baseline at 87.5% of each cell height. Standing head at approximately 22% of each cell height. All scarf tips, blade tips and feet must remain inside each cell with at least 5% clearance, even during the attack. No overlap between cells.
Animation order, left to right then top to bottom:
Row 1: idle ready stance; same idle stance breathing slightly; running right foot forward contact; running right foot forward compression.
Row 2: running right passing pose; running left foot forward contact; running left foot forward compression; running left passing pose. These six running frames form one coherent stride cycle, visibly alternating legs.
Row 3: airborne jump knees tucked; sword attack windup lifting dao behind shoulder; broad forward horizontal dao slash extended toward the right; sword attack follow-through lowered blade.
Row 4: explosive low forward dash with torso leaning toward the right, scarf trailing left; alternate low dash; brief upright hit recoil; calm ready stance.
Maintain consistent body scale and baseline, side-facing orientation, transparent padding and character identity in all 16 cells. No floating magical effects or slash arcs; the engine provides those separately.
